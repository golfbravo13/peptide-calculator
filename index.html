📖 How to use this calculator

① Dose — This is the amount of peptide you want per injection. Check your protocol for the recommended dose (e.g. 250 mcg = 0.25 mg).

② Strength — This is the total amount of peptide in your vial, printed on the label (e.g. 5 mg, 10 mg).

③ BAC Water — This is how much Bacteriostatic Water (BAC water) or sterile water you will inject into the vial to dissolve the peptide powder.

Result — The calculator tells you how many units to draw on a standard U-100 insulin syringe to get your desired dose.

💉 Individual Peptide Protocols

Retatrutide

GLP-1 / GIP / Glucagon
At 90 kg with stable weight, 2–4 mg/week is the sensible ceiling — metabolic benefit without the aggressive GI effects of clinical weight-loss doses (9–12 mg). Goal is recomposition, not weight reduction. Reassess at 8 weeks.

Inject: Subcut (abdomen/thigh/upper arm) · same day each week · no fasting · 30–60 min after a light meal reduces nausea during titration · keep protein ~180 g/day to protect muscle.

BPC-157

Repair & Recovery
Ideal for joint health, connective tissue repair and gut integrity — highly relevant training 5–6 days/week. Works locally, so injection site matters.

Site tip: For a specific joint/tendon issue, rotate injections near that site — BPC-157 has a strong local effect. TB-500 handles systemic coverage.

TB-500

Systemic Recovery
Works systemically — site doesn't need to be near the injury. Focus is systemic recovery, flexibility and connective-tissue resilience. 2.5 mg loading suits 90 kg; scaling to 5 mg is unnecessary here.

CJC-1295 (No DAC)

GHRH · GH Stack
A GHRH agonist — tells the pituitary to release growth hormone. Always stacked with Ipamorelin for a synergistic pulse. At 90 kg, 200 mcg is the sweet spot. ⚠ Fasting required — carbs/fat before injection blunt the GH response.

⚠ Fasting rule: inject at bedtime, ≥2–3 hrs after your last meal. Eat well during the day, then let the bedtime injection work in deep sleep.

Ipamorelin

GHRP · GH Stack
A selective GH-releasing peptide acting on a different receptor to CJC-1295 — together they produce a much larger combined GH pulse. Selective: doesn't meaningfully raise cortisol or prolactin. Same fasting rule.

GH Stack: draw CJC-1295 200 mcg (6 u) + Ipamorelin 200 mcg (6 u) into the same U-100 syringe = 12 units total. One bedtime injection, fasted. Vial 10 mg / 3 mL.
🧬 Signature Stacks

Wolverine Stack

BPC-157 + TB-500
The classic recovery & healing stack — BPC-157's local tissue repair paired with TB-500's systemic reach. Together they cover tendons, ligaments, muscle and gut. The go-to for injury rehab and hard training blocks.
ComponentDoseTimingFrequency
BPC-157250 mcg ×2AM + PM, 8–12 h apartDaily

Cycle: run 6–8 weeks, then 4 weeks off. Best times: BPC-157 morning & evening; TB-500 whenever convenient (Mon + Thu pairs well). Neither needs fasting — keep them separate from the bedtime GH stack only for injection comfort, not for efficacy. Note: BPC-157 is WADA-banned — avoid if drug-tested.

GLOW Stack

GHK-Cu + TB-500 + BPC-157
The skin, hair & aesthetic recovery stack. GHK-Cu drives collagen, skin quality and hair, while TB-500 + BPC-157 handle tissue repair. Often sold as a single blended 70 mg vial — reconstitute once, draw one shot.
